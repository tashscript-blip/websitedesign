Tactical Artificial System Hierarchy (TASH)

Self‑writing, infinite‑scaling Pyramid Mathematics.

Overview

TASH is an open‑software framework engineered for real‑time intelligence, autonomous adaptation, and recursive data processing. Built on a 10‑dimensional pyramid architecture, it evolves its own codebase to meet the demands of the most complex operational environments.

Key Features

  • 10‑Dimensional Pyramid Architecture – multi‑layered decision nodes
  • Self‑writing Codebase – adapts logic without human intervention
  • Real‑time Data Integration – ingests streams from sensors, APIs, and legacy systems
  • Infinite Horizontal Scalability – distributes seamlessly across clusters

Government & Defense Applications

TASH is built to meet federal security and compliance standards from the ground up. It supports mission‑critical operations with:

  • Zero‑trust architecture – data never leaves your controlled environment
  • Petabyte‑scale handling – designed for signals intelligence and satellite data
  • FedRAMP‑aligned controls – maps directly to NIST 800‑53

Available for evaluation under GSA Schedule and other federal procurement vehicles.
