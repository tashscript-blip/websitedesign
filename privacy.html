Privacy

Legacy Grove Codex LLC runs this site. This page explains what information this site collects — and what it does not.

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Contact: traheem75th@gmail.com

What this site collects

This site does not collect information from you automatically. There is no account, no login, and no payment form on this site.

Your Service Desk brief

When you fill in a brief at the Service Desk, your draft is saved in your own browser only (your browser's local storage), so it survives a reload. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

If you copy the brief and email it to us, we receive only what you choose to send in that email — the brief itself, your email address, and anything else you include. We use it to reply to your enquiry and to confirm the scope of a work order in writing.

Cookies, analytics, and trackers

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or external assets. The pages, styles, and scripts are served from this site alone.

What we do with information you send us

We use information you email to us only to answer you and to carry out work you have asked for. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it except where we are required to by law.

Your choices

You can clear a Service Desk draft at any time with the “Clear brief” button on the homepage, or by clearing your browser's stored data for this site. You can also email us to ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it.

Changes to this policy

If how this site handles information changes — for example, if a form that submits directly to us is added — this page will be updated, and the date above will change.
