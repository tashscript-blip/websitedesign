60‑Minute Digital Declutter

  • 0–15 min: Identify and close old accounts (forums, niche social sites).
  • 15–30 min: Delete unused apps from phone/tablet (not opened in 6 months).
  • 30–45 min: Remove duplicate photos and downloads to free cloud space.
  • 45–60 min: Update weak/repeated passwords and log them in your index.

Security Best Practices

  • 🔐 Enable 2FA Everywhere – Use authenticator apps or hardware keys (YubiKey) for critical accounts.
  • 🚫 Zero Digital Seed Phrases – Never store crypto seed phrases in digital notes, photos, or emails.
  • 📁 Encrypted Storage – If storing a digital copy, encrypt the drive with a strong, unique password.

Annual Review Checklist

  • Passwords – rotate compromised or aged credentials.
  • Accounts – add new banking, email, or subscription services.
  • Legal Docs – ensure Will & Power of Attorney are current.
  • Contacts – verify emergency contacts' phone numbers and emails.
  • Business – update changes in ownership or revenue streams.
